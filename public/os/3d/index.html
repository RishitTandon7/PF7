01 — Method

How I remove friction

  1. 01

    Observe

    Sit with the designers and watch where the hours actually go.

  2. 02

    Find the real problem

    Separate what is annoying from what is expensive.

  3. 03

    Test AI tools

    Try the models on real briefs and real files, not demos.

  4. 04

    Build simple

    The smallest tool that deletes the step. No dashboard nobody asked for.

  5. 05

    Document

    A short playbook so the next person never needs me in the room.

  6. 06

    Measure & iterate

    Minutes saved per task, tracked honestly and tuned every week.
